# Founder Discovery Kit: JBF (Just Be Funny)

## Assumption Tally
Assumption tally: supports 0 / undermines 0 / neutral 0

## Kill Condition
**Kill Condition:** If fewer than 3 out of 10 interviewees report actively changing their dating behavior or using workarounds (like sending memes/jokes first) due to app exhaustion within the last 30 days, the core premise that humor-first matchmaking solves an urgent pain point is disproven.

---

## Part 1 — The 10 Behavioral Interview Questions

### Movement 1: Uncovering Status Quo & Frustrations
1. Tell me about the last time you went on a date or chatted with someone from a dating app where you felt completely drained or bored. What happened?
2. Walk me through how you typically decide who to swipe right or message on a dating app today, step by step.
3. When was the last time a match or opening line actually made you laugh out loud or genuinely excited to reply? What did they say or do?

### Movement 2: Evaluating Current Workarounds & Alternatives
4. What are you currently doing or paying to find people with a similar sense of humor or banter style?
5. Tell me about the last time you sent a meme, joke, or funny prompt to break the ice with someone new. Where were you, and how did they respond?
6. What other apps, communities, or methods have you tried to find dates where personality mattered more than photos? Why did you stop using them?

### Movement 3: Quantifying Cost, Time, and Pain
7. When did dating app small talk ("Hey, how are you?") last waste a significant amount of your time or completely kill your motivation to keep swiping? Walk me through it.
8. Think about the last dating app subscription or feature you actually paid real money for. What was the exact moment or limitation that made you pull out your credit card?

### Movement 4: Validating Behaviors vs. Intentions
9. Tell me about a time someone's sense of humor completely changed your initial physical attraction to them. What happened?
10. When was the last time you deleted a dating app out of frustration? What was the final straw that caused you to uninstall it?
