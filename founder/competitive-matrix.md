# Decision Matrix & Landscape Map: JBF Dating (Just Be Funny)

## 1. Decision Dimensions (Outcome-Driven, Not Feature Checklists)
Instead of listing 50 checkboxes where an unbuilt app claims every row, this matrix scores alternatives on **five dimensions that drive actual switching and retention** for singles experiencing swipe and small-talk fatigue:

1. **Humor & Lore Banter Fit:** How naturally the experience surfaces shared humor style, subcultural/lore fluency, and playful back-and-forth early on.
2. **Time-to-First-Spark:** How quickly a user gets past empty swiping or generic small talk to an engaging, reciprocal exchange.
3. **Total Cost (Money + Hidden Time):** Subscription cost plus the hidden hours spent swiping dead ends, repeating stale openers, and managing low-signal chats `(assumption, confidence: medium)`.
4. **Local Liquidity & Match Density:** Enough active local users in the same city/scene so good banter actually leads to real conversations and meetups.
5. **Trust, Safety & Low Friction:** Psychological comfort, low awkwardness/judgment, and low switching friction from trying a new app.

### Honest Loss Check
JBF **wins** on *Humor & Lore Banter Fit* and *Time-to-First-Spark* (for banter-seeking users), ties/competes on *Total Cost* and *Trust/Friction*, and **clearly loses** on *Local Liquidity & Match Density* at launch compared to incumbents with years of network scale.

## 3. Two-Axis Landscape Map & Empty Quadrant

- **X-Axis:** *Banter & Humor-First Depth* (Low = photo/swipe or generic small talk $\rightarrow$ High = humor prompts, lore fluency & playful back-and-forth)
- **Y-Axis:** *Local Match Liquidity & Scale* (Low = small/niche or unseeded pool $\rightarrow$ High = dense, broad local dating pool)

### Quadrant Placement
- **Top-Left (High Liquidity, Low/Med Banter Depth):** *Tinder, Bumble, Hinge* — huge local pools, but conversations frequently stall in repetitive openers or safe small talk.
- **Bottom-Right (High Banter/Humor Fit, Low Local Liquidity):** *JBF Dating (Near-Term MVP)* — strong humor/banter thesis, but constrained at first by local density.
- **Top-Right (High Liquidity, High Banter Depth):** The prize zone for a dating app—requires proving both banter fit *and* local concentration.
- **Bottom-Left (Low Liquidity, Low Banter Depth):** Dead zone / unappealing combination.

### Empty Quadrant Analysis: Opening or Graveyard?
The **Top-Right** corner (high banter chemistry *plus* real local liquidity) is where mainstream apps under-deliver on playful, low-small-talk chemistry, while the **Bottom-Right** is where JBF starts. Whether **High Humor/Banter Fit** is a real **opening** rather than a **graveyard** depends on behavioral evidence—not just saying "I hate small talk": do singles actually complete humor prompts, trade multi-turn banter, and show up locally when given a humor-first flow? Until that is verified in discovery and prototype tests, local liquidity remains the primary risk to solve alongside the banter loop.
