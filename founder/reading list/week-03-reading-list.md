# Week 3 Reading List — Market Sizing, Positioning & Beachhead Strategy

- **The Lean Startup** by *Eric Ries* — for keeping market claims tied to tested learning rather than spreadsheet optimism.
- **Zero to One** by *Peter Thiel* — the argument for dominating a small, well-defined market first; directly relevant if your SOM came out small and your instinct is to broaden.
- **Crossing the Chasm** by *Geoffrey Moore* — why the beachhead segment, not the total market, determines early strategy.
- **Obviously Awesome** by *April Dunford* — market category choice as a positioning decision; pairs with the segment filters you chose for SAM.
