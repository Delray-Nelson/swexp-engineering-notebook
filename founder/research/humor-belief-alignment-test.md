# Research Test: Humor Style, Lore Fluency & Belief/Value Alignment

## 1. Contrarian Hypothesis (What I Am Testing)
- **Conventional caution:** A witty opener or shared inside reference can be faked briefly, and humor style alone may be noisy when inferring politics, deeper beliefs, or cultural alignment.
- **My contrarian hypothesis to test:** Distinct humor patterns and genuine fluency in shared cultural/subcultural lore *do* correlate meaningfully with real compatibility, shared values, and conversational spark—provided you test them across **multi-turn banter, unscripted callbacks, and shifting tone** rather than a single opening line.

---

## 2. Falsifiable Predictions
1. **Longevity of Mimicry:** Someone who only knows surface-level references or tries to fake a persona will show noticeable friction, repetitive/generic replies, or loss of rhythm within **3–5 exchanges or a tone shift**, whereas someone genuinely aligned will riff naturally and stay consistent.
2. **Signal Value of Humor + Lore:** Paired prompt-and-banter exchanges (where users respond to playful/subcultural setups and trade follow-ups) will produce **higher mutual reply rates and better perceived conversational fit** than generic small-talk openers.
3. **Belief & Cultural Alignment Clue:** While a single punchline can be ambiguous, observing how someone handles **irony, punchlines, and multi-turn banter** over time will reveal real alignment (or clash) in sensibility and social posture more reliably than static bio tags alone.

---

## 3. Experiment Design (Low-Cost Test for JBF Dating)

### Setup
- **Format:** Structured banter/prompt test (via a lightweight prototype, interactive prompt flow, or moderated test sessions with single adults / reachable peers).
- **Stimuli:** Mix of:
  1. Everyday playful/banter prompts (low-stakes, high-wit).
  2. Specific subcultural / inside-lore prompts (testing familiarity vs. surface guessing).
  3. A mild tone shift or follow-up twist (testing whether banter holds up past the first joke).

### Metrics to Track (Behavior Over Stated Opinions)
- **Prompt completion rate:** % of participants who answer with an actual joke/banter attempt rather than skipping or giving a one-word filler.
- **Multi-turn survival rate:** % of exchanges that stay engaging past **turn 3+** and across a **tone shift** (playful -> slightly serious/grounded -> playful again).
- **Lore authenticity indicator:** Whether follow-up riffs show genuine context/nuance vs. recycled clichés or missed callbacks.
- **Perceived spark / alignment rating:** Post-test score on *"felt natural and genuinely aligned"* vs. *"felt forced, scripted, or off."*

---

## 4. Decision Rules / Kill & Pivot Criteria
- **Supports my contrarian hypothesis if:** Multi-turn humor/lore banter produces noticeably higher engagement and perceived alignment than generic openers, and fakers/mismatches visibly diverge within a few turns or tone shifts.
- **Challenges my contrarian hypothesis if:** Participants regularly sustain convincing surface banter without real alignment, or if subcultural jokes create friction/misinterpretation that hurts match quality.
- **Next action:** Use the observed behavior (not hypothetical agreement) to refine JBF Dating's prompt mechanics and onboarding flow.
