# Value Proposition Canvas: JBF (Just Be Funny)

## Customer side

### Jobs
- Find someone attractive and compatible to go on a date with
- Break the ice without feeling awkward or cliché
- Sift through dating app profiles efficiently during downtime

### Pains (Ranked by severity, worst first)
1. **Dating app small talk exhaustion:** Endless cycles of "Hey," "How are you?", and weather talk that drain motivation and kill momentum before a date ever happens.
2. **Superficial photo bias:** Profiles with great photos but zero conversational personality, leading to awkward, dead-silent first dates.
3. **App fatigue and high churn:** Deleting dating apps every few months out of sheer frustration with repetitive interactions.
4. **Wasted subscription spend:** Paying for premium filters or boosts only to experience the exact same boring conversational loops.

### Gains
- Instant natural laughs or banter right out of the gate
- Finding someone whose humor matches yours before meeting up
- A low-pressure, playful way to connect instead of a formal interview style

---

## Fit map

- **Pain "Dating app small talk exhaustion" (rank 1)** <- Relieved by **Humor-first icebreakers & prompt-matching algorithms**
- **Pain "Superficial photo bias"** <- Relieved by **Bio-to-banter preview matching**
- **Gain "Instant natural laughs or banter right out of the gate"** <- Created by **Joke/meme prompt response openers**

### Orphan features
- [AI caption generator for your own photos] — *Cut: Solves a vanity metric rather than the core conversational pain.*

### Unserved pains
- [Burnout from ghosting after a good laugh] — *Implies that humor breaks the ice well, but retention requires ongoing engagement hooks beyond the first message.*

---

## Positioning statement

For **single adults suffering from dating app fatigue**, **JBF** is a **humor-first dating app** that **forces meaningful banter from message one**, unlike **Tinder or Hinge, which rely on static photos and generic small talk**.

---

## Three headlines

1. **"Swipe less. Laugh more."**
   * *Subheadline:* Meet people who actually know how to carry a funny, engaging conversation from message one.
2. **"Because 'Hey, how are you?' is a crime."**
   * *Subheadline:* The dating app where your sense of humor breaks the ice before your photos do.
3. **"Find your vibe, skip the small talk."**
   * *Subheadline:* Match with people who share your exact brand of humor.
